More than a barbershop –

Since our inception, we have worked with the idea of changing the barber industry by raising quality and giving the new generation of barbers opportunities to develop and take their place.

Today we have a salon in the heart of Addis Ababa and a team of talented barbers. Our goal is to define and shape modern barber culture – with high quality, creative entrepreneurship, and unique experiences for our clients.
